## What GEO is, and what it is not

Generative engine optimization is the practice of shaping your content so AI answer engines can find it, understand it and cite it. The EICTA guide from IIT Kanpur describes it as getting cited in AI-generated answers, alongside traditional SEO tools. Kantar's 2026 trends report makes a similar point and says brands should publish clear, structured, machine-readable content so models understand what they offer.

It is not a trick. There is no setting that forces an assistant to recommend you. The honest version is: be easy to understand, be specific, and be consistent everywhere you appear online.

## A practical GEO checklist

### 1. Say what you do in one plain sentence

Put a direct description on your homepage and about page: who you help, what you do, where you work. Avoid slogans that a model cannot turn into a fact.

### 2. Use the same name and details everywhere

Your name, business name, city, phone number and profile links should match across your website, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile and social accounts. Mixed names make it harder for a model to treat them as one business.

### 3. Add structured data

Mark up your organisation, services, articles and FAQs with schema.org JSON-LD so the facts are machine-readable. Keep the markup identical to what is visible on the page.

### 4. Answer real questions on the page

Write short question-and-answer sections for the things buyers ask: what it costs to get started, how long results take, how success is measured. Keep each answer self-contained so it can be quoted.

### 5. Back claims with sources and dates

Specific, dated, attributed statements are easier to trust than general ones. Name your sources, show when a page was last updated and say plainly what is your own result and what is industry data.

### 6. Keep the basics healthy

Fast pages, a sitemap, working internal links and clear headings still matter. GEO sits on top of these.

## How to measure it

Start with a baseline. Ask the main assistants the questions your buyers would ask, record whether and how your business appears, and repeat monthly. Then track what matters: enquiries, branded searches and where new leads say they heard about you. Mentions alone are not a business result.

This is the same discipline as any other channel. Jasper's 2026 survey found only 41% of marketers could prove AI ROI, down from 49% the year before. Among the teams that changed how they measure, 60% reported returns of 2-3x or higher, so build the measurement before you scale the effort.

## FAQ

### Is GEO replacing SEO?

Kantar calls GEO the emerging successor to SEO, but today the two share the same fundamentals: clear content, good structure and trustworthy claims. Keep doing SEO and treat GEO as the next layer on top.

### Can I pay to be recommended by an AI assistant?

Not directly. What you can do is make your business easy to find, understand and verify, and keep your information consistent across the web.

### How long does GEO take to show results?

There is no reliable timeline, and assistants change often. Set a baseline, make changes one at a time and review monthly rather than expecting a quick jump.

### Does GEO matter for a small local business?

Yes, particularly for "best X in my city" style questions. Clear location details, services and reviews help an assistant place you correctly.

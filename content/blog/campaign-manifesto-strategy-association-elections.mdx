<KeyTakeaways>

- Start from who votes and what they care about, not from slogans.
- A manifesto should be short, specific and checkable.
- Coordinate online and offline outreach, and follow all election and platform rules.

</KeyTakeaways>

This is a neutral, practical framework for the marketing side of an association election. It does not take a position on any candidate or panel. See also [election campaign marketing](/services/election-campaign-marketing/).

## The framework

### 1. Understand the electorate
Who is eligible to vote, how are they reached, and what do they care about? Association electorates are small and reachable, so listening comes before messaging.

### 2. Write a clear manifesto
A few specific, achievable commitments beat a long list of promises. Write each so a voter could check later whether it happened.

### 3. Build the message from the manifesto
One consistent message across posters, messages, meetings and social posts. Test wording with a few members before wide release.

### 4. Coordinate online and offline
Direct conversations, meetings and printed material, alongside digital outreach. Decide who owns each channel.

### 5. Capture supporters, with consent
Keep a simple list of supporters and volunteers, and state how their details will be used.

### 6. Follow the rules
Follow the election authority's rules and every platform's political-advertising policies. Check them before launch.

### 7. Review daily
Short daily notes on what was done, what responses came in and what is next.

## FAQ

### How long should a manifesto be?

As short as it can be while staying specific. A voter should be able to read it in a few minutes.

### Should campaigns use social media?

Where the electorate uses it, yes, alongside direct contact, and within platform rules.

### How do you measure an election campaign?

By reach, response and follow-up completed, reviewed daily, since the outcome only arrives on election day.

### Is supporter data collected with consent?

It should be, with a clear statement of how it will be used.

## Why most Meta ad tests teach you nothing

- **Several things change at once.** New image, new headline, new audience: if results move, you cannot say why.
- **No end condition.** Tests are stopped when someone gets nervous, not when there is enough data.
- **Wrong metric.** Click-through rate is easy to improve and often unrelated to lead quality.

## A simple test framework

### 1. Set the baseline

Before changing anything, record current cost per lead, lead volume and, if you can, how many leads turn into real conversations. Without a baseline, you cannot claim an improvement.

### 2. Write a hypothesis

Use this format: *"If we change [one variable], then [metric] will improve because [reason]."* For example: "If we lead with the course outcome instead of the fee, cost per lead will fall because the audience cares about outcomes first."

### 3. Change one variable

Pick one of: creative, headline or primary text, audience, offer, or lead form. Keep everything else identical. Meta's own A/B test tool can split audiences so the variants do not overlap.

### 4. Decide when the test ends

Agree the budget and duration up front. Do not stop early because one variant looks ahead on day two.

### 5. Judge on cost per lead and lead quality

A variant that is cheaper but produces leads nobody can reach is not a win. Ask sales for feedback on the leads from each variant.

### 6. Log and repeat

Keep a table like this:

| Test | Hypothesis | Variable | Result | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outcome-led headline beats fee-led | Headline | _fill in_ | _fill in_ |

Winners become the new baseline; losers still tell you something about your audience.

## What to test first

A reasonable order for most lead-generation accounts:

1. The offer and the first line of the ad
2. The creative format
3. The audience
4. The lead form length and questions

Offers and messages usually move results more than small design tweaks.

## FAQ

### How long should a Meta ads A/B test run?

Long enough for each variant to collect a meaningful number of results, and across a full week so weekday patterns are included. Meta's tool will indicate when a result is reliable. Decide the duration before you start.

### What should I test first on Meta ads?

Start with the offer and the opening line of the ad, then creative format, then audience. These generally have the biggest effect.

### Is the lowest cost per lead always the best result?

No. Check lead quality with your sales team. A slightly more expensive variant that produces more genuine enquiries is the better one.

### Can I run A/B tests on a small budget?

Yes, but run fewer tests, with larger differences between them, and give each enough time. Small differences are hard to detect on small budgets.
